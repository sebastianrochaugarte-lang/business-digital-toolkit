# Business Digital Toolkit

## Project Overview

Business Digital Toolkit is an open-source project focused on developing accessible digital tools for entrepreneurs and small businesses in Latin America.

## Problem

Small businesses often lack affordable tools to manage product catalogs, calculate commercial costs, determine profit margins and organize export operations.

## Objectives

- Simplify product catalog creation.
- Automate cost and pricing calculations.
- Provide practical international trade tools.
- Improve digital adoption among small businesses.

## Planned Modules

1. Digital Product Catalog
2. Business Cost Calculator
3. Export Toolkit

## Target Users

Entrepreneurs, small businesses, independent sellers and emerging exporters in Latin America.

## Development Roadmap

- Phase 1: Project architecture and documentation.
- Phase 2: Development of the first functional modules.
- Phase 3: Testing and improvements.
- Phase 4: Documentation and public release.

## Technology

The project will explore accessible web technologies and AI-assisted development.

## Status

Early development stage.
